# Gikachbot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Gikachbot is a non-commercial Telegram bot that processes links voluntarily
submitted by its users.

## Data processed

When a person uses Gikachbot, the service may temporarily process:

- the Telegram user ID and chat ID needed to answer the request;
- URLs and commands sent to the bot;
- public post text, author information and media needed to complete the
  request;
- Telegram file identifiers used by result buttons and short-lived result
  reuse; and
- operational information such as request time, source platform, processing
  status and error category.

Gikachbot does not ask users for passwords, payment information or private
messages from other chats.

## Purpose

Data is processed only to complete a user-requested action, return the result,
provide optional media or translation actions, prevent abuse and diagnose
technical failures.

Gikachbot does not sell or license personal information, use it for targeted
advertising, build user profiles, or use submitted content to train artificial
intelligence or machine-learning models.

## Retention

Downloaded media exists on the bot server only while a request is being
processed. Temporary files are removed when processing succeeds, fails, times
out or is cancelled.

Minimal callback metadata, such as the submitted URL, attribution information
and Telegram file identifiers, may be cached for no longer than 24 hours and
is then removed automatically.

Telegram may retain messages and files delivered through its platform under
Telegram's own privacy policy. Users control messages in their Telegram chats
and may delete them through Telegram.

## External services

Submitted links may be sent to the relevant source platform to complete the
requested action. Telegram processes messages and files needed to deliver the
result.

Translation occurs only when the user explicitly requests it. The text may
then be sent to an external translation provider, such as Google Translate or
MyMemory, under that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

## Reddit

Official Reddit Data API access is not currently enabled. Any future approved
integration will be limited to individual public, non-restricted posts whose
URLs are explicitly submitted by a user. The proposed scope is described in
[REDDIT_DATA_ACCESS.md](REDDIT_DATA_ACCESS.md).

## Sharing

Information is shared with third-party services only when technically
necessary to perform an action requested by the user, such as Telegram
delivery, source-platform access or optional translation. It is not sold to
data brokers or advertisers.

## Deletion and questions

Short-lived service metadata is removed automatically within 24 hours.
Questions or deletion requests concerning data still controlled by Gikachbot
may be sent to [3953574@gmail.com](mailto:3953574@gmail.com). A GitHub issue
may be used for general questions, but Telegram IDs and other personal
information must not be placed in a public issue.
